
# Northstar Electronics — Fulfillment Policy

Policy ID: NS-FUL-001
Version: 1.0
Status: Active
Last Updated: 2026-10-16

## 1. Warehouse Fulfillment

- Existing warehouse inventory should be prioritized before purchasing additional units from suppliers.
- Warehouse stock must be verified before proposing an order.
- Warehouse fulfillment requires at least 1 business day for processing before shipment.

## 2. Supplier Fulfillment

- External suppliers may be used when warehouse inventory is insufficient.
- Supplier availability, unit cost, and lead time must be verified before including supplier units in a fulfillment plan.
- Supplier lead time represents procurement time and does not include final delivery transit.
- Supplier orders above $10,000 in procurement cost require manager approval.

## 3. Budget Restrictions

- A proposed order must not exceed the customer's stated budget without explicit customer approval.
- Product subtotal calculations must use the current catalog selling price.
- Taxes and shipping costs must be disclosed as additional or pending charges when not yet calculated.

## 4. Delivery Commitments

- Delivery dates must not be guaranteed without confirmed inventory, processing time, and shipping estimates.
- Orders requiring supplier procurement must account for supplier lead time.
- Requests with unverified delivery feasibility must be flagged for review.

## 5. Human Approval

- Every proposed fulfillment plan requires human approval before execution.
- Over-budget orders require customer confirmation before approval.
- Orders must not reserve inventory, issue purchase orders, or charge customers before authorization.
